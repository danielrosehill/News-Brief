# Daily Brief — trailing 24 hours

A digest of the last 24 hours, organised by subject. Fetch with `--brief daily`.

## Structure

```
# Daily Brief: <package name>
<DD MMM YYYY>, covering <from HH:MM> <day> to <to HH:MM> <day> (<timezone>)

## Top stories
3–5 items. One or two sentences each: what happened, who said it, why it matters.
Ranked by significance, not recency. Corroborated multi-outlet stories first.

## <Subject>
...

## Also noted
One-line items that did not make a section.

Sources: <n> of <m> feeds returned items. <gaps, if any>
```

## Rules

- Subjects are chosen from the day's material. Typical Israel set: Security & defence; Diplomacy; Politics & government; Gaza / Lebanon / Iran and the region; Economy; Society & legal. Drop empty subjects; never write "nothing to report" sections.
- Order within a section: significance, then recency.
- Each item ends with the outlets in parentheses: "(ToI, JPost)". Links in text mode go on the outlet names.
- Length: 400–700 words in text mode. A quiet day is a short brief.
- No analyst assessment section. Context sentences are fine; forecasts belong in a SITREP.
