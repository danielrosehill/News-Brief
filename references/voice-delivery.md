# Voice delivery

Used when the brief will be spoken: a voice assistant asked for it, the user said "read it to me", or `--voice` was passed.

- Plain sentences. No markdown, bullets, links, URLs, tables or emoji.
- Open with the time: "Here's the news as of twenty past one." Then the content.
- Times in speakable form ("about forty minutes ago", "at half past twelve"), not "12:30+02:00".
- Say outlet names in full the first time ("the Times of Israel"), not "ToI".
- Spell out abbreviations a listener might not parse; keep IDF, US, UN as they are.
- One idea per sentence, under 25 words. Pause between stories with a new sentence, not a list marker.
- Lengths: Breaking about 60–90 seconds (150–220 words); Daily about 2–3 minutes (300–450 words, top stories plus one line per subject); SITREP reads BLUF and assessment only (about 90 seconds), then asks "Want the full sections?".
- Close with one line: "That's the latest" or the offer to go deeper on a story. No sign-off pleasantries.
